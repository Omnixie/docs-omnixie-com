DS Downloader Privacy Policy

Effective date: [9/1/2026]

DS Downloader ("the app") is published by Omnixie LLC. This policy describes what the app does with your information. The short version: we collect nothing.

Data collection

The app collects no personal data, no usage analytics, and no diagnostics. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that phone home.

Where your information lives

  • NAS credentials (username and password) are stored only in your device's Keychain, protected by iOS. They are sent only to the NAS address you configure, to sign in to Synology DiskStation Manager.
  • Device list and app settings are stored only on your device.
  • All network traffic goes directly from your device to your own Synology NAS. The app has no servers of its own and never proxies, mirrors, or inspects your downloads.

Local network access

The app asks for Local Network permission to discover NAS devices on your network via Bonjour. Discovery results are shown to you and are not transmitted anywhere.

Notifications

Download-complete notifications are generated locally on your device. No push notification service is used.

Purchases

The optional "DS Downloader Pro" upgrade is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app does not target children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
